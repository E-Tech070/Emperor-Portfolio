Our Story

Cooking From the Heart Since 2023

Prissy's Kitchen started as a small pot of Jollof rice in Lagos State. Prissy's Kitchen cooked for neighbours, then for the whole street, and eventually for the city.


Today, we serve hundreds of guests daily — but every dish is still made with the same love, the same family recipes, and the same belief that good food brings people together.
